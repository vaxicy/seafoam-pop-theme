Seafoam Pop is a soft, light Chrome theme built for calm, unhurried browsing. A fresh seafoam green frame wraps a pale mint tab strip and a gentle lavender-white toolbar, so the browser feels airy without ever fighting the pages you open.

The palette stays quiet on purpose: deep teal text keeps every label easy to read, while the light surfaces hold still behind your work. If you like a workspace that feels refreshed rather than decorated, this one settles in from the first tab.
